The anatomy of a scam

5 stages of manipulation

Every Aviator-style scam follows the same playbook. Understanding each stage is the first step to protecting yourself.

STAGE 01

The honeymoon phase

You join, make small bets, and win consistently. The algorithm is programmed to let you win. You feel confident and increase your bets.

STAGE 02

Bot crowd manipulation

Fake player accounts populate the screen, appearing to cash out at high multipliers. This is theater — they are controlled by the platform to build your confidence.

STAGE 03

Large bet = early crash

When you place a big bet, the server detects it and sets the crash point below 1.3x — before the round starts. You have almost no time to react.

STAGE 04

Withdrawal blocked

When you try to withdraw winnings, sudden KYC requests, "technical issues," and account suspensions appear. Your money is trapped.

STAGE 05

The chase loop

After losing, you're offered "bonuses" and "free bets" with impossible wagering requirements. You keep playing to recover losses that were never meant to be recovered.

PROOF

What "provably fair" means

Legitimate platforms publish a cryptographic hash of the crash point before each round. You can verify it after. If a platform doesn't show this — the game is rigged by design.

Warning signs

Red flags to walk away from

If you see any of these on a betting platform, stop and leave immediately.

⛔

No provably fair proof

No cryptographic hash published before each round means the crash point can be anything the server wants, at any time.

🔒

Withdrawal suddenly blocked

KYC requests, "technical issues," or account suspension that appear only when you try to withdraw a large amount.

🤖

Suspiciously active players

Dozens of players always online, always winning big — these are bots run by the platform to create false social proof.

📱

APK-only app

Not available on Google Play or the App Store? Side-loaded APKs have no oversight and can manipulate game data directly on your device.

🎁

Impossible bonus terms

"Deposit ₹1,000 get ₹5,000 bonus" with a 50x wagering requirement means you must bet ₹2.5 lakh before withdrawing a rupee.

📢

Telegram "insider tips"

Anyone promising fixed match tips or guaranteed crash points is running a classic social engineering scam. There are no insiders.
